# gale_bat (바람박쥐)

## Identity

- Bipedal bat humanoid debuffer, natural 1★
- Dark fur body with white fluffy neck ruff, large pointed bat ears
- Glowing element-colored eyes, sharp fang snarl
- Wide spread leathery bat wings with silver-tipped bone spurs
- Dark ornate chest-and-belt armor with diamond gem buckle
- Tattered dark cloak panels hanging from waist
- Sharp three-claw hands and bird-like clawed feet
- Aggressive forward lunge stance with one claw extended
- Circular elemental energy ring swirling around the body
- **Keep across elements**: bat proportions, wing spread, ear shape,
  armor layout, gem buckle, cloak panels, energy ring motion
- **Change per element**: eye glow, gem color, energy ring hue, cloak trim only

## artKey

`gale_bat_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber-red eyes, ruby gem, ember energy ring, charred cloak edges
- **water**: teal eyes, sapphire gem, water-vortex energy ring, cool blue trim
- **wind**: green eyes, emerald gem, classic gale energy ring, green trim
- **light**: gold eyes, topaz gem, radiant holy energy ring, pale gold trim
- **dark**: violet eyes, amethyst gem, void curse energy ring, purple trim

## Awaken delta

- Same silhouette with larger wing span and taller ear crown
- Gem buckle evolves into twin-core crystal; energy ring denser
- Cloak gains ornate rune trim; wing bone spurs longer
- Portrait: gale_bat_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: gale_bat_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, ears, gem buckle, extended claw, wing leading edge readable
- **back**: wing membrane backs, cloak rear panels, tail if any, armor back
- Back must not mirror the front
