# venom_stinger (독침벌)

## Identity

- Armored biomechanical wasp debuffer, natural 1★
- Symmetrical insect body: rounded thorax, large segmented abdomen, six legs
- Metallic purple-bronze chitin plates with gold trim bands
- Four translucent wings with elemental vein glow and wispy edge aura
- Large glowing compound eyes, curved segmented antennae, pointed head crest
- Two spherical side orb sensors in gold rings flanking the head
- Large pincer-like forelegs and four sharp spindly rear legs with gold tips
- Long glowing stinger at abdomen tip with chevron energy marks
- Hovering combat stance slightly above ground, symmetrical front presentation
- **Keep across elements**: wasp anatomy, wing count, orb sensors, pincer forelegs,
  abdomen segmentation, stinger silhouette, purple-gold base plating
- **Change per element**: eye glow, wing veins, abdomen chevrons, stinger aura only

## artKey

`venom_stinger_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eyes, ember wing veins, orange stinger glow and heat wisps
- **water**: teal eyes, blue-green wing veins, droplet mist at stinger
- **wind**: emerald eyes, classic green wing veins and toxic green mist
- **light**: gold eyes, pale-yellow wing veins, soft holy radiance on orbs
- **dark**: violet eyes, void-green wing veins, shadow spores and curse mist

## Awaken delta

- Same wasp silhouette with taller crest and heavier gold chitin pauldrons
- Abdomen chevrons evolve into a brighter core channel; stinger grows longer
- Orb sensors gain secondary rings; wing aura becomes denser but controlled
- Portrait: venom_stinger_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: venom_stinger_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: eyes, crest, orb sensors, pincers, wing leading edges, stinger readable
- **back**: rear wing membrane layout, abdomen segments, stinger base, leg backs
- Back must not mirror the front
