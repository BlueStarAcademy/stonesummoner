# glacier_mage (빙하마법사)

## Identity

- Regal ice sorceress, debuffer role, natural 4★
- Pale skin, long flowing white-silver hair, glowing elemental eyes
- Tall pointed ice-crystal crown integrated into a white fur-lined hood
- Blue scale-mail bodice with silver filigree and central chest gem
- Voluminous white fur-trimmed sleeves with jagged crystal spikes on outer edges
- Layered ice-tabard skirt with snowflake embroidery and thick white fur hem
- Sharp crystalline greaves and pointed armored boots
- Left hand raised, cradling a large floating jagged ice crystal cluster; right hand open for casting
- **Keep across elements**: pale skin, white hair, hood-crown silhouette, scale bodice,
  fur-trim robe layering, crystal cluster prop, regal upright stance
- **Change per element**: eye glow, crystal color, embroidery, scale tint and restrained aura only

## artKey

`glacier_mage_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eyes, warm ember crystals on ice armor seams, charred fur tips
- **water**: teal-blue eyes, classic glacier blue crystals, frost mist and wet highlights
- **wind**: emerald eyes, pale cyan crystals, sparse ice shards and airy ribbons
- **light**: gold eyes, ivory-gold crystal crown accents, soft holy frost radiance
- **dark**: violet eyes, black-blue crystals, void frost and restrained shadow spores

## Awaken delta

- Same silhouette with taller multi-spike ice crown and heavier crystal pauldrons
- Larger floating crystal cluster with secondary orbiting shards
- Longer fur mantle and ornate scale tabard, brighter seam glow
- Portrait: glacier_mage_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: glacier_mage_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, crown, crystal cluster, chest gem, skirt layers and boots readable
- **back**: hood, crown rear spikes, mantle, sleeve crystal backs, skirt panels and fur hem
- Back must not mirror the front or turn the face toward camera
