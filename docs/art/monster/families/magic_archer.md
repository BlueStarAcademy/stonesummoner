# magic_archer (마법궁수)

## Identity

- Elf archer attacker, natural 2★
- Long flowing hair, pointed ears, stern focused expression
- Ornate green-and-gold plate armor with tiered spaulders
- Large flowing dark cape with gold embroidery
- Oversized magical recurve bow of crystalline element energy
- Element arrow nocked and drawn; dynamic archer stance
- **Keep across elements**: elf proportions, hair length, armor layout,
  cape shape, bow silhouette, archer pose
- **Change per element**: eye glow, bow energy, gem accents, arrow glow only

## artKey

`magic_archer_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eyes, magma crystal bow, ruby chest gems
- **water**: teal eyes, aqua crystal bow, sapphire gems
- **wind**: green eyes, emerald crystal bow, jade gems
- **light**: gold eyes, radiant crystal bow, topaz gems
- **dark**: violet eyes, void crystal bow, amethyst gems

## Awaken delta

- Bow evolves into larger twin-prong crystal frame; cape gains rune trim
- Spaulders taller with second gem row; arrow becomes split-tip energy bolt
- Portrait: magic_archer_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: magic_archer_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, bow, chest gems, cape front readable
- **back**: hair, cape rear, quiver if any, armor back panels
- Back must not mirror the front
