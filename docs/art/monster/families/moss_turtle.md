# moss_turtle (이끼거북)

## Identity

- Ancient colossal tortoise guardian, tank role, natural 1★
- Low, extremely broad quadruped stance with thick pillar legs and blunt claws
- Weathered mountain-like shell made from overlapping dark stone plates
- Living moss, short vines and small fern growth follow shell seams
- Stern reptilian face, heavy brow, short hooked beak and thick scaled neck
- Bronze rune collar and one central shield-shaped chest plate; no handheld weapon
- **Keep across elements**: olive-black scales, mountain shell structure, moss coverage,
  broad body proportions, collar and chest plate
- **Change per element**: eye glow, shell-seam energy, rune light and small elemental
  growths only; never recolor the entire turtle or replace moss with a full effect cloud

## artKey

`moss_turtle_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: orange eyes, ember cracks between stone plates, sparse charred moss tips
- **water**: teal eyes, wet stone highlights, tiny shell-edge rivulets and blue lichen
- **wind**: green eyes, lifted fern tips, a few leaves and restrained air ribbons
- **light**: gold eyes and runes, pale flowers, warm highlights on bronze and stone
- **dark**: violet eyes and runes, black-blue stone seams, sparse shadow spores

## Awaken delta

- Same body and shell silhouette with a taller central shell ridge
- Evolved bronze-gold rune collar, larger chest shield and carved shell-edge bands
- Ancient tree stump or crystal crown rooted into the shell center, element-specific glow
- Denser but controlled moss and brighter shell-seam energy
- Portrait: moss_turtle_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: moss_turtle_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: head, both front legs, chest shield and front half of shell readable
- **back**: dedicated rear 3/4 view showing the full mountain-shell layout, rear legs,
  short tail, rune bands and vegetation placement
- Back must not be a mirrored front and must not turn the head toward the camera
