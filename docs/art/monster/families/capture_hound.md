# capture_hound (포획사냥개)

## Identity

- Armored hellhound capturer, natural 2★
- Large muscular Doberman-like dog, black-tan markings, fierce glowing eyes
- Heavy dark wrought-iron plate armor: faceplate, spiked collar, pauldrons, chest, greaves
- Thick conical armor spikes glowing with element fire/energy
- Element flame and embers from armor gaps and spike tips
- Menacing square front battle stance, all four legs visible
- **Keep across elements**: dog proportions, armor piece layout, spike count,
  Doberman silhouette, front stance
- **Change per element**: eye glow, spike glow, armor gap flames only

## artKey

`capture_hound_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eyes, orange-red spike flames, ember gaps
- **water**: teal eyes, aqua spike glow, cool mist gaps
- **wind**: green eyes, lime spike glow, airy spark gaps
- **light**: gold eyes, radiant spike glow, holy flame gaps
- **dark**: violet eyes, void spike glow, curse flame gaps

## Awaken delta

- Larger pauldrons and longer spikes; faceplate gains rune etching
- Collar evolves twin-ring; flame aura denser halo around body
- Portrait: capture_hound_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: capture_hound_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: faceplate eyes, collar spikes, chest plate readable
- **back**: armor back plates, tail, hind greave backs, spike rear
- Back must not mirror the front
