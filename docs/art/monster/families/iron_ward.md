# iron_ward (철벽수호)

## Identity

- iron ward guardian, natural 2★ tank
- Body: stocky armored humanoid knight
- Focus: tower shield and short mace
- Armor/cloth: heavy riveted iron plate
- Combat stance: shield wall brace
- **Keep across elements**: tower shield shape, riveted plate, stocky build
- **Change per element**: metal tint, rune glow on shield

## Awaken delta

- winged shield crest, thicker pauldrons
- Portrait: iron_ward_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: iron_ward_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## artKey

`iron_ward_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: warm red-orange-gold accents and aura
- **water**: teal-blue-silver accents and aura
- **wind**: green-lime accents and airy aura
- **light**: gold-white holy radiance accents
- **dark**: violet-black void accents and aura

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, primary weapon/focus, chest readable
- **back**: rear armor, cape/wings/tail if any, weapon back — not a mirror flip
- Full body grounded, feet visible, ~12% empty margin, square 1:1
- Magenta `#FF00FF` plate OR true transparent alpha — no checkerboard, no text
