# cinder_imp (불씨임프)

## Identity

- Small demonic imp attacker, natural 1★
- Compact muscular imp body (not oversized chibi head), lava-crack skin
- Flame hair crown, curved horn pair with glowing seam cracks
- Large fierce orange eyes, pointed ears, mischievous fang smirk
- Dark volcanic-rock armor on shoulders, chest, belt and shins
- Bright diamond gems in chest, belt buckle and shin guards
- Long thin tail with arrow tip; large clawed hands and feet
- Aggressive forward clawing combat stance
- **Keep across elements**: imp proportions, horn layout, armor pieces,
  gem placement, tail, clawed limbs, flame-hair silhouette
- **Change per element**: skin crack glow, gem color, flame hair and aura only

## artKey

`cinder_imp_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: orange-yellow flame hair, amber gems, magma crack glow
- **water**: teal flame-like vapor hair, blue gems, cool steam crack glow
- **wind**: green-lime flame hair, emerald gems, airy spark crack glow
- **light**: gold-white flame hair, pale-gold gems, holy radiance cracks
- **dark**: violet-black flame hair, purple gems, void curse crack glow

## Awaken delta

- Same imp silhouette with taller horn crown and heavier shoulder armor
- Gems evolve into brighter twin-core crystals; tail gains small flame fin
- Flame hair becomes denser crown; armor trim more ornate
- Portrait: cinder_imp_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: cinder_imp_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, horns, chest gem, claw hand, shin guards readable
- **back**: horn backs, tail, rear armor plates, flame hair from behind
- Back must not mirror the front
