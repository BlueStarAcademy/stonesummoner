# frost_witch (서리무녀)

## Identity

- Ice mage debuffer woman, natural 2★
- Pale skin, long white hair, cold composed expression, glowing element eyes
- Deep blue form-fitting gown with high slit, silver armor plates
- Blue crystal crown tiara; massive fur-lined dark blue cloak
- Tall silver staff with giant glowing element crystal spearhead in filigree cage
- Swirling elemental pool at feet with droplets and particles
- Regal caster stance one hand raised
- **Keep across elements**: woman proportions, hair, gown layout, crown,
  cloak fur trim, staff silhouette, pool base
- **Change per element**: eye glow, crystal color, pool hue, gown accents only

## artKey

`frost_witch_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eyes, ruby crystal staff, ember pool wisps
- **water**: teal eyes, sapphire crystal staff, classic water pool
- **wind**: green eyes, emerald crystal staff, airy ripple pool
- **light**: gold eyes, topaz crystal staff, radiant holy pool
- **dark**: violet eyes, amethyst crystal staff, void mist pool

## Awaken delta

- Crown evolves into taller crystal crown; cloak mantle longer
- Staff crystal grows twin-prong; pool gains ornate ripple ring
- Portrait: frost_witch_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: frost_witch_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, crown, staff crystal, gown and pool readable
- **back**: hair, cloak rear fur lining, staff shaft back, pool rear
- Back must not mirror the front
