# thunder_spear (번개창병)

## Identity

- Dark armored lancer attacker, natural 2★
- Full spiky plate armor in charcoal steel with gold filigree trim
- Angular helm with glowing element eye slits; purple hair plume from crest
- Massive ornate polearm spear with jagged glowing element blade
- Lightning or element energy crackling around spearhead
- Tattered purple waist cape and loincloth panels
- Heroic forward lancer stance
- **Keep across elements**: armor spike layout, helm shape, spear type,
  gold trim pattern, cape panels, lancer pose
- **Change per element**: eye glow, spear energy, lightning color only

## artKey

`thunder_spear_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eyes, magma spear glow, ember crackle
- **water**: teal eyes, aqua spear glow, water arc crackle
- **wind**: green eyes, gale spear glow, air spark crackle
- **light**: gold eyes, radiant spear glow, holy lightning crackle
- **dark**: violet eyes, void spear glow, curse crackle

## Awaken delta

- Taller helm plume, heavier layered pauldrons, twin-ring chest emblem
- Spear blade grows larger with rune cage; energy crackle denser
- Portrait: thunder_spear_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: thunder_spear_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: helm, spearhead glow, chest plate, cape front readable
- **back**: plume, cape rear, pauldron backs, spear shaft rear
- Back must not mirror the front
