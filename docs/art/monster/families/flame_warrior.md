# flame_warrior (화염무사)

## Identity

- Demonic samurai attacker, natural 3★
- Ornate dark-gold O-yoroi armor with lava cracks glowing through fissures
- Horned kabuto helm with demonic mask face and element eye glow
- Dual long swords wreathed in element flame energy, pointed downward stance
- Tattered dark waist cape spreading wide triangular silhouette
- **Keep across elements**: samurai proportions, kabuto horns, armor layout,
  dual-sword pose, cape shape
- **Change per element**: eye glow, lava crack hue, sword flame color only

## artKey

`flame_warrior_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: orange lava cracks, amber sword flames, ember horn tips
- **water**: teal crack glow, aqua sword wisps, cool mist horns
- **wind**: green crack glow, gale sword trails, airy horn sparks
- **light**: gold crack glow, radiant sword light, holy horn shine
- **dark**: violet crack glow, void sword flames, curse horn mist

## Awaken delta

- Taller horn crown, heavier sode pauldrons, twin-ring chest emblem
- Sword flames denser with rune bands; crack glow intensifies
