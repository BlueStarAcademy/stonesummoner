# dew_slime (이슬슬라임)

## Identity

- Cute round translucent slime blob, support role, natural 1★
- Semi-spherical jelly body with internal bubbles and glossy surface droplets
- Two large shiny expressive eyes, tiny gentle smile
- Single leaf-or-droplet sprout on top of head with thin stem
- Sitting on a shallow elemental puddle base with ripples and splash arcs
- Soft healing aura motes; calm grounded pose (not bouncing)
- **Keep across elements**: round blob silhouette, eye layout, sprout stem,
  puddle base shape, bubble count, cute support stance
- **Change per element**: body glow tint, sprout leaf color, puddle ripple hue only

## artKey

`dew_slime_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: warm amber-orange inner glow, ember-tinted sprout, lava-ripple puddle
- **water**: deep blue-teal inner glow, aqua leaf sprout, classic water puddle
- **wind**: green-lime inner glow, fresh leaf sprout, airy ripple puddle
- **light**: gold-white inner glow, pale lotus sprout, radiant holy puddle
- **dark**: violet-black inner glow, shadow-leaf sprout, void mist puddle

## Awaken delta

- Same blob silhouette with taller crown sprout and denser bubble core
- Puddle base gains ornate ripple ring; eyes brighter with star glints
- Body glow intensifies; sprout evolves into twin-leaf or lotus crown
- Portrait: dew_slime_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: dew_slime_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: eyes, smile, sprout, front bubble highlights, puddle splash readable
- **back**: rear blob curve, sprout stem from behind, puddle rear ripples
- Back must not mirror the front
