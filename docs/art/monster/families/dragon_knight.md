# dragon_knight (고룡기사)

## Identity

- Elite dragon-blood knight, attacker role, natural 5★
- Tall human male warrior in heavy jagged dragon-scale plate armor
- Horned draconic helm with narrow visor; pale stern face partially visible
- Charcoal-black scale plates with sharp obsidian edges and layered pauldrons
- Large tattered dragon wings with membrane glow and a long torn battle cape
- Massive two-handed volcanic greatsword with magma core in the blade
- Magma-orange light bleeds through armor seams at chest, belt, knees and blade
- Grounded wide combat stance, sword tip near the ground in the right hand
- **Keep across elements**: human knight body, horned helm, scale armor layout,
  wing and cape structure, greatsword silhouette, wide grounded stance
- **Change per element**: seam glow, wing membrane energy, blade aura and pauldron trim only

## artKey

`dragon_knight_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: orange-red magma seams, ember wing membranes, volcanic blade glow
- **water**: teal-blue seam light, wet metal highlights, frost-ice blade core
- **wind**: emerald-lime seam energy, airy wing trails, sharp wind-etched blade
- **light**: gold-white holy seam radiance, bright wing membranes, radiant blade core
- **dark**: violet-black void seams, shadow mist on wings, curse-glyph blade aura

## Awaken delta

- Same silhouette with evolved horned crown-helm and heavier wing armature
- Blade grows longer with split magma/element core and ornate crossguard
- Cape becomes armored mantle; pauldrons gain dragon-skull trim
- Brighter controlled elemental aura without hiding the body
- Portrait: dragon_knight_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: dragon_knight_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: helm face slit, chest seams, sword, wings leading edge and boots readable
- **back**: wing membrane structure, cape attachment, rear scale plates, sword spine visible
- Back must not mirror the front
