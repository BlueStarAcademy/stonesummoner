# herb_alchemist (약초술사)

## Identity

- Gnome herbalist support, natural 2★
- Small stature, childlike face, bushy white beard, long white ponytail with cords
- Large glowing element eyes; ornate green-white tunic with gold leaf embroidery
- Leather belts and pouches with glowing vials; woven plant basket on back
- Wooden mortar and pestle in one hand, bubbling potion flask in other
- Swirling green element energy with floating leaves around body
- Cheerful grounded support stance
- **Keep across elements**: gnome proportions, beard ponytail, tunic pattern,
  basket, mortar and flask tools, support stance
- **Change per element**: eye glow, potion color, energy wisps, vial hue only

## artKey

`herb_alchemist_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eyes, warm ember potion, flame wisps
- **water**: teal eyes, aqua potion, mist wisps
- **wind**: green eyes, emerald potion, leaf gale wisps
- **light**: gold eyes, radiant potion, holy spark wisps
- **dark**: violet eyes, void potion, curse spore wisps

## Awaken delta

- Larger basket with exotic blooms; potion flask evolves twin-chamber
- Tunic gains ornate rune embroidery; energy aura denser
- Portrait: herb_alchemist_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: herb_alchemist_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, mortar, flask, pouches, basket strap readable
- **back**: ponytail, basket plants, tunic rear, pouch backs
- Back must not mirror the front
