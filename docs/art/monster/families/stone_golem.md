# stone_golem (돌골렘)

## Identity

- Chunky rock golem tank, natural 1★
- Humanoid body built from large dark-gray stone blocks with moss patches
- Broad shoulders, thick pillar legs, enormous clenched fists, no weapon
- Glowing rune bands on chest, forearms and shins; energy in stone cracks
- Small fierce glowing eyes under heavy stone brow
- Stone loin-guard plate at waist; wide stable defensive stance
- **Keep across elements**: blocky stone anatomy, moss patches, rune band layout,
  fist tank silhouette, no handheld weapon
- **Change per element**: crack glow, rune light and small wisps only

## artKey

`stone_golem_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: orange magma cracks, ember runes, heat wisps at shoulders
- **water**: teal-blue seam glow, wet stone highlights, frost runes
- **wind**: green-lime crack energy, airy rune light, sparse leaf motes
- **light**: gold-white holy runes, warm bright seam glow
- **dark**: violet-black void cracks, curse runes, restrained shadow spores

## Awaken delta

- Same blocky silhouette with taller crown of stacked stone plates
- Heavier rune bands and brighter controlled elemental core in chest
- Denser moss and carved shoulder spikes, stronger crack glow
- Portrait: stone_golem_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: stone_golem_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face eyes, chest rune, fists and leg blocks readable
- **back**: rear stone plate stacking, rune bands, moss and heel blocks
- Back must not mirror the front
