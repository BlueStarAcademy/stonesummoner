# forest_sprite (숲요정)

## Identity

- Youthful forest elf druid, support role, natural 1★
- Slim agile body (not chibi), large expressive eyes, pointed elf ears
- Hair of soft green strands mixed with thin leaves, small white lotus flowers
- Living leaf-and-vine chest harness with central glowing gem
- Layered sharp leaf skirt, vine leg wraps, bare feet with visible toes
- Twin short twisted-wood wands with elemental glow at tips
- Floating petals and restrained nature motes around hands
- **Keep across elements**: elf proportions, leaf armor layout, twin wands,
  bare feet, flower hair accents, support caster stance
- **Change per element**: gem glow, wand flame, embroidery and petal accents only

## artKey

`forest_sprite_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber gem, warm ember wisps on wands, charred leaf tips
- **water**: teal gem, droplet ribbons and cool dew highlights
- **wind**: emerald gem, classic green nature glow, leaves and airy ribbons
- **light**: gold gem, pale flower radiance, soft holy motes
- **dark**: violet gem, shadow spores on vines, muted purple leaf edges

## Awaken delta

- Same silhouette with crown of blooming flowers and longer leaf mantle
- Wands gain carved rune bands and brighter twin elemental cores
- Chest gem grows into a floral sun emblem
- Portrait: forest_sprite_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: forest_sprite_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, ears, gem, wands and leaf skirt readable
- **back**: hair vines, mantle leaves, skirt layers, wand backs visible
- Back must not mirror the front
