# stone_fist (석화장사)

## Identity

- Stone golem martial artist tank, natural 2★
- Hulking muscular humanoid body of cracked dark-grey rock plates
- Glowing element energy in every body fissure; spiral glyph on forehead and shoulders
- Heavy dark prayer-bead necklace with gold tassel; thick frayed yellow waist sash
- Spiked gold wrist gauntlets; blocky stone feet; fists clenched wide stance
- **Keep across elements**: golem proportions, rock plate layout, spiral glyphs,
  bead necklace, sash, gauntlets, martial stance
- **Change per element**: fissure glow color, eye glow, glyph hue only

## artKey

`stone_fist_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber magma fissure glow, ember eyes
- **water**: teal aqua fissure glow, cool mist eyes
- **wind**: green lime fissure glow, airy spark eyes
- **light**: gold radiant fissure glow, holy eyes
- **dark**: violet void fissure glow, curse eyes

## Awaken delta

- Heavier shoulder rock plates; glyphs evolve into ornate rune circles
- Sash and beads gain metallic trim; fissure glow brighter and denser
- Portrait: stone_fist_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: stone_fist_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face glyph, fists, beads, sash knot readable
- **back**: rock plate backs, sash ends, shoulder glyphs from behind
- Back must not mirror the front
