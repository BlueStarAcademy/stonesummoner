# blaze_hound (작열사냥개)

## Identity

- blaze hunting hound, natural 2★ attacker
- Body: lean muscular canine, flame mane, charcoal fur
- Focus: burning fangs and claw gauntlets
- Armor/cloth: molten leather collar and limb wraps
- Combat stance: pounce crouch
- **Keep across elements**: hound proportions, mane shape, collar
- **Change per element**: flame mane color, eye glow

## Awaken delta

- armor plating on shoulders, larger mane crown
- Portrait: blaze_hound_{element}_awaken.webp

## artKey

`blaze_hound_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: warm red-orange-gold accents and aura
- **water**: teal-blue-silver accents and aura

- **light**: gold-white holy radiance accents

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, primary weapon/focus, chest readable
- **back**: rear armor, cape/wings/tail if any, weapon back — not a mirror flip
- Full body grounded, feet visible, ~12% empty margin, square 1:1
- Magenta `#FF00FF` plate OR true transparent alpha — no checkerboard, no text
