# seal_apprentice (봉인견습)

## Identity

- Young male mage stonesage, natural 2★
- Messy dark hair, glowing element amber eyes, serious focused expression
- Ornate black-purple layered robe with gold filigree trim and structured hood
- Glowing diamond gems on chest, belt, boots and robe hems
- Right hand raised palm up beneath floating element energy orb with star core
- Purple magical lining patterns on cloak interior visible at edges
- Grounded caster stance, left arm at side
- **Keep across elements**: young man proportions, hair, robe layering,
  hood shape, gem placement, orb-casting pose
- **Change per element**: eye glow, orb color, gem hue, lining glow only

## artKey

`seal_apprentice_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eyes, ember orb, ruby gems
- **water**: teal eyes, aqua orb, sapphire gems
- **wind**: green eyes, emerald orb, jade gems
- **light**: gold eyes, radiant holy orb, topaz gems
- **dark**: violet eyes, void curse orb, amethyst gems

## Awaken delta

- Hood gains jeweled diadem; orb evolves twin-ring seal sigil
- Robe trim more ornate with floating rune glyphs; gems become twin-core
- Portrait: seal_apprentice_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: seal_apprentice_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, orb, chest gem, hood and robe front readable
- **back**: hood rear, hair, robe panels, boot backs
- Back must not mirror the front
