# crow_scout (까마귀정찰)

## Identity

- Anthropomorphic raven humanoid capturer, natural 1★
- Black feathered body with sharp beak and glowing element eyes
- Hooded tattered dark cloak over shoulders
- Crisscross leather chest straps with metal rings
- Leather belt with square glowing buckle and side pouches
- Ragged loincloth panel; leather bracers and greaves with rune engravings
- Large taloned hands and bird claw feet
- Crouched predatory forward stance, claws spread
- Ethereal energy wisps from eyes, shoulders and claw tips
- **Keep across elements**: raven proportions, hood cloak, strap layout,
  belt buckle shape, bracer greave placement, crouched scout stance
- **Change per element**: eye glow, buckle color, rune glow, energy wisps only

## artKey

`crow_scout_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eyes, ruby buckle, ember rune glow, flame wisps
- **water**: teal eyes, sapphire buckle, cool rune glow, mist wisps
- **wind**: green eyes, emerald buckle, classic gale rune glow, air wisps
- **light**: gold eyes, topaz buckle, holy rune glow, radiant wisps
- **dark**: violet eyes, amethyst buckle, void rune glow, curse wisps

## Awaken delta

- Same silhouette with taller hood crown and broader wing-feather mantle
- Buckle evolves into twin-core crystal; runes brighter on all armor
- Cloak gains ornate trim; talons longer with energy trails
- Portrait: crow_scout_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: crow_scout_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: beak, hood, buckle, bracer runes, extended talons readable
- **back**: cloak rear, feather mantle, belt pouch backs, greave backs
- Back must not mirror the front
