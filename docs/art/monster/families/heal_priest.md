# heal_priest (치유사제)

## Identity

- Adult human priestess healer, support role, natural 2★
- Calm oval face, long ash-blonde hair and a deep ceremonial hood
- Layered ivory robe with charcoal inner lining, fitted silver-bronze torso armor,
  long split sleeves and pointed embroidered hems
- Tall one-handed healing staff with a winged circular head and central crystal
- Rigid sun-halo frame mounted behind the shoulders; not free-floating
- Soft leather boots remain visible beneath the robe for a grounded stance
- **Keep across elements**: face, ash-blonde hair, ivory robe base, hood shape,
  staff silhouette, shoulder-mounted halo and robe layering
- **Change per element**: crystal, eye light, embroidery, metal trim and restrained
  healing particles; never recolor all cloth or change her into an attacker

## artKey

`heal_priest_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber crystal, red-gold embroidery, warm restorative flame motes
- **water**: teal crystal, blue-silver trim, droplets and cool healing ribbons
- **wind**: emerald crystal, green-gold stitching, leaves and soft air ribbons
- **light**: pale-gold crystal, bright ivory-gold trim and restrained holy motes
- **dark**: violet crystal, black-silver trim and controlled restorative shadow wisps

## Awaken delta

- Same face, body and staff silhouette with more ornate fitted torso armor
- Evolved double-ring shoulder halo, jeweled hood diadem and longer embroidered mantle
- Staff head gains a second wing layer and larger central crystal
- Four short spectral ribbon-wings emerge from the halo without hiding the robe
- Portrait: heal_priest_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: heal_priest_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face, staff head, chest emblem, both sleeves and boots readable
- **back**: halo mounting bracket, hood and hair, mantle, sleeve backs and robe panels visible
- Back keeps the staff in the same hand and is never a mirrored front
