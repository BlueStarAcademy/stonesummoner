# shadow_thief (암영도적)

## Identity

- Hooded assassin debuffer, natural 2★
- Deep hood with metallic crest, masked lower face, glowing element eyes
- Dark ornate plate over leather with sharp wing-like pauldrons
- Glowing gem on chest and belt; large tattered dark cloak
- Dual curved daggers with element energy along inner blade edge
- Purple-black shadow flame wisps from shoulders and weapons
- Crouched ready combat stance
- **Keep across elements**: hood silhouette, mask, pauldron wings,
  dual dagger layout, cloak, assassin stance
- **Change per element**: eye glow, gem color, blade energy, shadow wisps only

## artKey

`shadow_thief_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eyes, ruby gems, ember blade energy, flame wisps
- **water**: teal eyes, sapphire gems, cool blade energy, mist wisps
- **wind**: green eyes, emerald gems, airy blade energy, gale wisps
- **light**: gold eyes, topaz gems, radiant blade energy, holy wisps
- **dark**: violet eyes, amethyst gems, void blade energy, curse wisps

## Awaken delta

- Taller hood crest, heavier wing pauldrons, twin-core chest gem
- Daggers gain rune etched hilts; shadow aura denser
- Portrait: shadow_thief_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: shadow_thief_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: hood face, gems, both daggers, pauldrons readable
- **back**: cloak rear, hood back, pauldron wing backs, belt rear
- Back must not mirror the front
