# mace_soldier (철퇴병)

## Identity

- Heavy armored knight tank, natural 2★
- Massive dark plate armor with spiked pauldrons and angular full helm
- Glowing element slits in helm eyes; cracked armor seams with inner glow
- Oversized spiked morning-star mace with elemental flame/aura on head
- Tattered dark waist cloth panels; wide grounded tank stance
- **Keep across elements**: knight proportions, helm shape, spike layout,
  mace type, pauldron silhouette, grounded stance
- **Change per element**: eye glow, armor crack glow, mace aura only

## artKey

`mace_soldier_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: amber eye slits, magma crack glow, flaming mace head
- **water**: teal eye slits, cool mist crack glow, water-vortex mace
- **wind**: green eye slits, airy spark crack glow, gale mace wisps
- **light**: gold eye slits, radiant crack glow, holy light mace
- **dark**: violet eye slits, void crack glow, curse flame mace

## Awaken delta

- Taller helm crest, heavier layered pauldrons, twin-ring chest emblem
- Mace head grows larger with rune band; crack glow intensifies
- Portrait: mace_soldier_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: mace_soldier_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: helm face, pauldrons, mace head, chest plate readable
- **back**: helm back, cloak panels, pauldron backs, mace shaft rear
- Back must not mirror the front
