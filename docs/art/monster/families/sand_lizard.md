# sand_lizard (모래도마뱀)

## Identity

- Bipedal lizardman warrior attacker, natural 1★
- Sandy tan scaled body with serrated head frills
- Glowing element-colored narrow eyes, sharp fang jaw
- Tattered wrap tunic with dark geometric patterns
- Leather belt with circular glowing emblem buckle
- Curved falchion with magma-edge glow; round wooden shield with metal plates
- Leather forearm and shin wraps, clawed three-toed feet
- Grounded wide battle stance, sword forward shield ready
- **Keep across elements**: lizard proportions, frill layout, tunic pattern,
  belt emblem shape, sword and shield types, wrap placement
- **Change per element**: eye glow, emblem color, blade edge glow, aura wisps only

## artKey

`sand_lizard_{element}` — fire / water / wind / light / dark

## Element variants

- **fire**: orange eyes, amber emblem, magma blade edge, ember shoulder wisps
- **water**: teal eyes, sapphire emblem, cool water blade edge, mist wisps
- **wind**: green eyes, emerald emblem, airy blade edge, sand-dust wisps
- **light**: gold eyes, sun emblem, radiant blade edge, holy spark wisps
- **dark**: violet eyes, void emblem, curse blade edge, shadow mist wisps

## Awaken delta

- Same silhouette with taller frill crown and heavier shoulder plates
- Emblem evolves into twin-ring sun sigil; blade gains rune band
- Shield metal plates more ornate; tunic trim brighter
- Portrait: sand_lizard_{element}_awaken.webp
- Battle: sand_lizard_{element}-awaken-front.webp / -awaken-back.webp

## Front/back requirements

- **front**: face frills, emblem, sword edge glow, shield front readable
- **back**: frill backs, cloak or tunic rear, tail, shield back strap
- Back must not mirror the front
